Main projectv0.2

          
Shadowfax

          
A security operations platform that treats AI agents as first-class identities. It gives you visibility, investigation and explainability over what agents actually do, with a rule-based detection engine, policy management, risk scoring and a live dashboard polling alerts every few seconds.

          
Detection stays deterministic and policy-driven. LLMs explain, correlate and summarise; they never act as the security boundary.
